# Gina, a personal assistant

Gina is a private, personal AI assistant built on the open-source Hermes agent framework. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Gina connects to the owner's own Google accounts to read email and calendar events, prepare a short weekly summary, and send that summary by email after the owner approves it. Gina may also help the owner with documents, spreadsheets and files in the owner's own Google Drive.

The Google sign-in apps that Gina uses are named Hermes Agent and Social Gina - GMail Read-Only.
