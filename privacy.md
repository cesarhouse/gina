# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Gina is a private, personal AI assistant operated by one individual for their own use. This policy explains how Gina handles Google user data.

## What data Gina accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Gina may access the owner's Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Contacts, and Google Drive, Docs and Sheets files, limited to the permissions the owner approves on Google's consent screen. Gina accesses only the owner's own Google accounts.

## How the data is used

Gina uses this data only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as preparing a weekly summary of email, calendar events and messages, and sending emails that the owner has reviewed and approved.

## Storage and sharing

Data is processed on computers controlled by the owner, and sign-in tokens are stored only on those computers. Gina does not sell Google user data and does not share it with anyone, except the AI model services Gina uses to carry out the owner's requests. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

## Limited Use

Gina's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke Gina's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
